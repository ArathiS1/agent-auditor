# Safety and privacy


Remove personal data such as emails and phone numbers from logs before storing them. Prompt injection is when text in a document tells the model to ignore its rules; treat retrieved text as untrusted.

Give tools the least permission they need, and use read-only tools where possible. Keep an audit log of who changed a prompt and when.

Ask users before sending their files to an outside API. Rotate any key that has been pasted into a chat or committed to git.
