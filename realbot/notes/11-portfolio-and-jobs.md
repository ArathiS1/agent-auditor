# Portfolio and job search


A portfolio project should solve a real problem and show tests, a demo and a short write-up. Put a one-line result at the top of the README, such as the accuracy you measured.

Aim to apply to 5 roles a day and tailor the first paragraph of each application. Prepare two stories for interviews: one project you finished and one failure you learned from.

Practice explaining a system design on one page: inputs, components and failure modes. Keep a weekly study log of 3 things learned.
